Entry for [One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73) on make.fun.

`main.py` writes a one-line office joke to `/output/entry.txt`. It is a
first-person confession built on the canonical office joke — the meeting
that should have been an email: I say our Monday 9 a.m. meeting could've
been an email, so my boss makes me send it — 47 slides to all 12 of us,
with a read receipt. One character, one scene, and the turn lands in the
final clause: the email turns out worse than the meeting.

Run: `python3 main.py` — writes `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 132 characters,
inside the 200-character gate).
